Our Mission & Standards

Modern software ecosystems are increasingly crowded with proprietary tracking, bundled adware, and opaque data collection policies. AppDirectory was founded to provide developers, privacy advocates, and everyday users with an accessible directory of software where source code is verifiable and user rights are prioritized.

Curation Criteria

Every tool featured on AppDirectory must satisfy three rigorous baseline criteria: verifiable license status under recognized OSI/Fsf licenses, active development maintenance within the past 12 months, and absence of dark patterns or mandatory telemetry.

Editorial Independence

We do not accept sponsored placements, paid rankings, or promotional compensation. All project descriptions are authored by our editorial contributors with direct references to official repositories.
