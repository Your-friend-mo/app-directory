Overview & Architecture of Obsidian Notes

Core Principles & Design

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my notes locked into Obsidian?

No. All notes are saved as plain text Markdown files in local folders on your storage drive, ensuring perpetual access.

Does Obsidian work completely offline?

Yes. Obsidian requires no internet connection to view, edit, search, or organize your knowledge base.

Is Obsidian free for personal use?

Yes. Obsidian is 100% free for personal and educational use with no artificial limits on note counts.
